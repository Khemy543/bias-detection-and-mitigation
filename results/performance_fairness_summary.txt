Performance and Fairness Metrics — All Models and Mitigation Phases
=====================================================================
Dataset : UCI Adult Income (adult_recruitment.csv) — 45,175 rows
Target  : Selected = 1 if income >$50K/year (proxy for hired/promoted)
          Selected = 0 if income ≤$50K/year
Positive rate: ~24.8%  |  Gender split: Male 67.5%, Female 32.5%

Mitigation phases averaged over 5 random seeds (42–46).
Baseline is a single seed (seed=42).

Fairness metric interpretation:
  DI  (Disparate Impact)            — higher is fairer; legal threshold ≥ 0.80  ✓
  DPD (Demographic Parity Diff.)    — lower is fairer; 0 = perfect parity
  EOD (Equalized Odds Diff.)        — lower is fairer; 0 = perfect equality

Key findings:
  - Starts with severe gender bias (DI=0.190, far below the 0.80 legal threshold).
  - Post-processing surpasses the legal DI threshold (0.836 ≥ 0.80):
      DPD drops 85.6%  (0.279 → 0.040, p < 0.001)
      EOD drops 83.6%  (0.371 → 0.061, p < 0.001)
      DI  rises 362%   (0.181 → 0.836, p < 0.001)
  - Recall drops post-mitigation (0.617 → 0.493): more selective per group, fewer
    true positives selected overall — the expected fairness-recall trade-off.
  - Precision stays stable (0.612 → 0.604): candidates selected remain high quality.
  - ROC AUC unchanged — threshold shifts do not affect the underlying score ranking.

Key findings:

  - Post-processing delivers the best DPD reduction of all three models:
      DPD drops 88.6%  (0.252 → 0.029, p < 0.001)
      EOD drops 75.0%  (0.300 → 0.075, p < 0.001)

  - Notable post-process pattern: Precision rises (0.620 → 0.693) while Recall
    drops sharply (0.617 → 0.403). RF applies higher thresholds for the
    majority group (Male), selecting fewer but higher-confidence candidates —
    this is the group-specific threshold at work.
  - Accuracy effectively unchanged across all phases (p=0.098).
  - Best model for minimising accuracy cost while meeting the DI ≥ 0.80 threshold.

All three models cross the DI ≥ 0.80 legal threshold after post-process mitigation.
ROC AUC is unchanged across phases — confirming threshold optimisation does not
alter the model's discriminative power, only how that power is applied per group.
The precision-recall trade-off after mitigation is expected: equalising selection
rates across groups requires raising thresholds for the over-selected group (Male)
and lowering them for the under-selected group (Female), which shifts the
precision-recall balance rather than degrading overall model quality.
